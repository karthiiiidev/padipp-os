ATTENDANCE CALCULATOR

Attendance Buffer & 75% Shortage Formula

Students can input classes conducted, classes attended, and their required minimum percentage to determine their exact standing. PADIPP.OS calculates whether you have a safe buffer or are in shortage without relying on approximations.

  • Safe Missable Classes: ⌊Attended / Req% − Conducted⌋
  • Required Recovery Classes: ⌈(Req% × Conducted − Attended) / (1 − Req%)⌉
  • Instantly test attendance scenarios before taking planned leaves.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to common questions about PADIPP.OS, its mathematical logic, and data privacy.

What is PADIPP.OS?

PADIPP.OS is a student academic dashboard designed to help college and university students track attendance, forecast internal assessment marks, solve for required exam scores, manage class timetables, and monitor exam deadlines in one place.

Calculations are executed client-side in the browser according to the student's own academic regulations, without sending academic records to external servers.

How does the attendance calculator work?

You enter your total conducted classes, attended classes, and required minimum attendance percentage (typically 75% or 80%).

PADIPP.OS uses discrete integer formulas to evaluate your exact buffer margin: floor(Attended / ReqPercent - Conducted). If your attendance is below the threshold, it calculates the consecutive classes you must attend to recover: ceil((ReqPercent * Conducted - Attended) / (1 - ReqPercent)).

Can PADIPP.OS calculate how many classes I can miss?

Yes. When your attendance percentage is above your required cutoff, each subject card displays your exact safe margin (e.g. "Can miss 3 classes safely"). The calculation updates instantly as you log classes.

Can I calculate the exam marks required for a target grade?

Yes. Enter your scored internal marks (such as Midterms, Assignments, and Attendance) and select your target letter grade (S, A, B, C, etc.). The predictor calculates the precise semester exam score needed to achieve the total percentage required for that grade.

Can I customize my university's grading system?

Yes. Through the Settings tab, students can configure their minimum attendance percentage, maximum internal assessment marks, university end-semester exam weightings, and specific grade boundary percentages.

Does PADIPP.OS work on mobile?

Yes. PADIPP.OS is built mobile-first. It is fully responsive across smartphone screen sizes (including compact 320px devices), tablets, and desktop displays, complete with an app bottom navigation bar.

Does PADIPP.OS work offline?

Yes. PADIPP.OS is an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). All application assets are cached via a Service Worker, and academic data is stored directly on your device inside browser IndexedDB. You can use it without an active internet connection.

Is an account or login required?

No. PADIPP.OS does not require accounts, logins, emails, or passwords. Your academic records stay exclusively on your device. You can export or restore complete JSON backups anytime from Settings.

Where is PADIPP.OS from?

PADIPP.OS is a student-focused productivity project from Trivandrum, Kerala, designed to serve engineering, science, and arts college students across India and globally.
